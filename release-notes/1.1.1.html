Halfpast 1.1.1

  • Quieter updates. When a new version is ready, a small arrow appears on the notch and an Update button in the ribbon, instead of a window that could open behind your other apps.
  • Check for updates and Quit without right-clicking. Both are now at the bottom of Places & appearance; in the ribbon, press u to check and ⌘Q to quit. (Menu bar managers like Ice can take over the right-click on the notch.)
